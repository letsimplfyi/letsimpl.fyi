Goals

  1. Educate. Use already available materials that's existing in multiple platforms fragmented into pieces and self learn and teach each other to use, diagnose and fix all items u own and use.
  2. Organise, Like minded individuals who are irked by all the complexities and are suffocated by the ways of this world.
  3. document. make as much as printed and digital archives, guidebooks, manuals hack books etc and a catalogue for all kinds of products that we can reclaim.

Mission

simpler world where tech need not be hostile and working against the interests of the users so that the companies that produce them can benifit off from it.
